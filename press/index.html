About the game

Moonlight Inn is a cozy fantasy café game. Each night, travellers, townsfolk and curious night guests wander into your little inn under the moon, and you brew them warm, magical drinks by hand.

Pour from the bottles, grind herbs in a mortar, steep tea, press fresh fruit and warm the cup on the hearth stone. Every guest wants something different, and some only hint at a feeling. Learn their stories, find their secret tastes, and grow an empty plot into a legendary inn.

Features

  • Nearly 200 recipes made by hand: pour, grind, brew, press, shake, heat and cool
  • Guests with stories: regulars, wandering travellers, a fairy, a ghost and more, each with hearts to win and memories to unlock
  • Grow your inn from an empty plot through a garden, a cabin and a café to a legendary inn
  • Moon phases, weather, four seasons and festivals
  • The Moonlight Post: a morning paper full of town gossip
  • Liora, your cheerful partner behind the counter
